itaOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

itaOS helps businesses in Tanzania understand which of their Facebook and Instagram ads lead to paying customers on WhatsApp. This policy explains what data itaOS handles and why.

Who is responsible

Each business that connects its accounts is the data controller for its own customers' data. itaOS processes that data on the business's instructions, as a data processor under the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

What we collect

  • Account data: the email address you sign in with.
  • Advertising data: read-only performance figures for the ad accounts you connect (spend, impressions, chats started, ad names).
  • WhatsApp data: for the WhatsApp Business number you connect, the messages exchanged with customers, their WhatsApp number and profile name, and ad referral information Meta attaches to chats started from an ad. With your approval during setup, up to six months of past chats are imported once.
  • Orders and expenses you record or import: amounts, dates, status, delivery status, optional product and notes, and who recorded or changed them (kept as an audit trail). When you import a CSV, customer phone numbers in the file are used only to find the matching chat inside your own workspace; they are not stored and never used to match records across businesses.
  • Business profile and content records: products, prices, delivery areas, payment methods, tone, common questions and constraints you enter, and any post performance figures you record.
  • AI-assisted chat analysis and drafts: up to 80 recent message excerpts per conversation, each truncated to 400 characters, may be sent to Anthropic to produce conversation labels, short evidence quotes, summaries and suggested next steps. When a team member asks for a follow-up draft, that chat and the business profile are sent the same way; when they ask for content ideas, the business profile, recorded post figures and counts of customer objections are sent (no message text). itaOS removes detected phone numbers, payment codes and email addresses before sending text, but automated redaction can miss personal information. Kito is instructed not to infer health, religion or other sensitive traits and not to make product-effectiveness claims. Do not import information you are not authorized to process.

How we use it

itaOS uses the data to show the business its own results: cost per confirmed sale per ad, where customer conversations stop, reply times, follow-ups and objections. AI-assisted analysis is used to summarize and classify chats; it does not send messages to customers, publish content, change ad budgets or confirm sales. Drafts are shown to the business to edit and send themselves; only orders a person records or imports count as sales. itaOS does not sell data or use one business's data for another business.

We do not sell your data or your customers' data, and we do not use one business's data for another business.

Storage and security

Data is stored in an encrypted database (Supabase, hosted in the EU). Access tokens are kept in an encrypted vault. Each business's data is separated by access rules so only its members can see it.

Chat excerpts used for AI-assisted analysis are sent to Anthropic's API after the redaction described above. The connected business should tell its customers about this processing where required and obtain any necessary permissions. Data may therefore be processed by service providers outside Tanzania; the business should review applicable transfer safeguards and contractual terms before connecting customer data.

Retention and deletion

A business can disconnect at any time from Meta Business Settings or by contacting us, and can ask for its data to be exported or deleted. We delete it within 30 days of a verified request. To request deletion of data about you as a customer of a business, contact that business or us.

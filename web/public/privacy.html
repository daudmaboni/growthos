GrowthOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

GrowthOS helps businesses in Tanzania understand which of their Facebook and Instagram ads lead to paying customers on WhatsApp. This policy explains what data GrowthOS handles and why.

Who is responsible

Each business that connects its accounts is the data controller for its own customers' data. GrowthOS processes that data on the business's instructions, as a data processor under the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

What we collect

  • Account data: the email address you sign in with.
  • Advertising data: read-only performance figures for the ad accounts you connect (spend, impressions, chats started, ad names).
  • WhatsApp data: for the WhatsApp Business number you connect, the messages exchanged with customers, their WhatsApp number and profile name, and ad referral information Meta attaches to chats started from an ad. With your approval during setup, up to six months of past chats are imported once.
  • Sales you record: amounts you confirm as paid.
  • AI-assisted chat analysis: up to 80 recent message excerpts per conversation, each truncated to 400 characters, may be sent to Anthropic to produce conversation labels, summaries and suggested next steps. GrowthOS removes detected phone numbers, payment codes and email addresses before sending text, but automated redaction can miss personal information. Do not import information you are not authorized to process.

How we use it

GrowthOS uses the data to show the business its own results: cost per confirmed sale per ad, where customer conversations stop, reply times, follow-ups and objections. AI-assisted analysis is used to summarize and classify chats; it does not send messages to customers or make sales confirmations. GrowthOS does not sell data or use one business's data for another business.

Storage and security

Data is stored in an encrypted database (Supabase, hosted in the EU). Access tokens are kept in an encrypted vault. Each business's data is separated by access rules so only its members can see it.

Chat excerpts used for AI-assisted analysis are sent to Anthropic's API after the redaction described above. The connected business should tell its customers about this processing where required and obtain any necessary permissions. Data may therefore be processed by service providers outside Tanzania; the business should review applicable transfer safeguards and contractual terms before connecting customer data.

Retention and deletion

A business can disconnect at any time from Meta Business Settings or by contacting us, and can ask for its data to be exported or deleted. We delete it within 30 days of a verified request. To request deletion of data about you as a customer of a business, contact that business or us.
